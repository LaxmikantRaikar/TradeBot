Findidng Breakeven condition for short trading:
--------------------------------------------------------
After entry, any candle after alert candle, whose high is below alert candle, then it becomes Breakeven Track Candle(BTC).

After BTC is set, there are four conditions to check with next candle(NC):
	1. If NC.low < BTC.low and NC.high < BTC.high then NC will be our new BTC.
	2. If NC.low > BTC.low and NC.high > BTC.high then BTC is confirmed as existing BTC.
	3. If NC.low > BTC.low and NC.high < BTC.high then BTC remains same.
	4. If NC.low < BTC.low and NC.high > BTC.high then NC will be our new BTC.

If any candle after confirmed BTC breaks low of Confirmed BTC then stop loss is moved to entry.
